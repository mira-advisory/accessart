# 08 — Delivery Agents for AccessArt: the outsourced layer

*Research date: 30 August 2026. Market: South East Queensland (Brisbane / Gold Coast / Sunshine Coast). All prices AUD unless noted. Companion to `06-delivery-and-swap-logistics.md` (couriers, packaging, node model); this document covers the **agent layer** — gig platforms, owner-drivers, white-glove networks and the eventual in-app delivery-agent role.*

## Executive summary

The mainstream on-demand networks are legally closed to artwork: Uber's Courier product prohibits fragile items outright and caps value at $100, DoorDash Drive is a food-and-grocery pipe, and GoPeople's included FreightSafe warranty *explicitly excludes works of art and glass* — so even where a gig driver will physically carry a wrapped canvas, damage cover is zero. The two platforms that actually work today are **Airtasker** (Brisbane delivery tasks show a median of ~$112, with a real Newstead artwork-load task at $300, and no art prohibition — but only third-party liability insurance, not goods cover) and **owner-driver "ute and a mate" franchises** like Little Green Truck (from $30/job, Brisbane-wide). Sherpa remains the only credible on-demand API partner after absorbing Drive Yello in 2025, but its cover terms are unpublished. The economics only become rational when moves are **batched**: a weekly swap-day van run drops the cost per artwork from ~$100 (per-job gig pricing) to roughly $10–$30 at 10–24 artworks per run — the same consolidation logic the fine-art shuttles (TED/IAS, Artwork Transport) already run at gallery grade. Legally, the ground shifted **this month**: the Fair Work Commission's first employee-like-worker minimum standards order commenced 17 August 2026 (food/grocery delivery only, min $31.30–$32.00/hr), and TWU applications to extend standards to *last-mile goods delivery* are live — meaning the moment AccessArt runs its own dispatch feed and pays independent agents, it becomes a "digital labour platform" with statutory obligations. Recommendation: book per-job via Airtasker/local van operators now, build a preferred-agent panel with agreed rates and evidenced insurance next, and only cross into an in-app `delivery_agent` role with legal advice and volume that clears the batching threshold.

## 1. On-demand / gig delivery options that can (and can't) carry art

### 1.1 Airtasker — the workhorse ✅

Airtasker is the only mainstream gig marketplace with **no item-type prohibition on artwork** and deep evidence of art/furniture moves in Brisbane.

- **Verified Brisbane task pricing** (live examples on Airtasker's own pages): general delivery tasks in Brisbane run **$74–$170 with a median of $112** ([Brisbane delivery](https://www.airtasker.com/au/services/delivery/brisbane/)); furniture delivery runs **$104–$250, median $150** ([Brisbane furniture delivery](https://www.airtasker.com/au/services/furniture-delivery/brisbane/)). Real tasks: *"Artwork, TVs, office equipment… barstools, table, chairs"* Newstead → Hendra for **$300** (mixed load from a storage unit); a 700×900×500mm rolling cabinet Newstead → New Farm with moving blankets for **$80**; a blanketed dining table Highgate Hill → Ascot (8km) for **$110**; a 2.4m sofa Fortitude Valley → Bulimba for **$60**. Airtasker's national furniture-delivery guide puts typical jobs at **$60–$150** ([cost guide](https://www.airtasker.com/au/services/furniture-delivery/)).
- **Realistic price for a wrapped 60×90cm framed canvas, Brisbane metro:** **$60–$110 per point-to-point job** (it's a one-person, one-car task at the bottom of the delivery range).
- **Insurance — the catch:** Airtasker Insurance is a **third-party liability policy (up to $10m AUD)** covering a Tasker's liability for personal injury or property damage to others during a platform-booked task; it excludes faulty workmanship and only applies to tasks booked and paid on-platform ([Airtasker insurance overview](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-au/articles/23363113132057-What-do-I-need-to-know-about-Airtasker-s-Insurance-Coverage), [customer guide](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-au/articles/217998398-How-does-third-party-liability-insurance-on-Airtasker-work-A-guide-for-customers)). It is **not goods-in-transit cover**, and liability policies commonly exclude property in the worker's care, custody or control — so treat the carried artwork as **uninsured unless the individual Tasker holds their own transit cover** (some do; ask). This needs verification against the policy wording before relying on it.
- **Vetting/ratings:** ID badges, an ACIC national **Police Check Badge** via provider Sterling RISQ (point-in-time check, clean-record requirement) ([police check badge](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-au/articles/234693667-What-is-the-Police-Check-Badge-How-do-I-get-one)), licence/qualification badges, public ratings and completion rates. Payment escrowed on-platform.
- **Fees:** Tasker service fee **12.5–20% tiered** + poster connection fee from **$4.90 (capped $49.50)** ([fee analysis](https://kingtradie.com/airtasker-review/), [Airtasker on service fees](https://www.airtasker.com/blog/service-fees-providing-context-transparency/)).
- **API:** none for dispatch — it's a consumer marketplace; ops must post tasks manually. Status: **operating, AU-wide, strong Brisbane density** (423 delivery Taskers listed for Brisbane).

### 1.2 Uber (Connect / Courier / Direct) — closed to art ❌

- **Uber Courier/Connect (consumer):** prohibits **fragile items** outright, caps item value at **$100 AUD** ($500 with store pickup), 20kg combined, and prohibits "items of irreplaceable sentimental value" ([Uber AU restrictions](https://www.uber.com/au/en/blog/direct-courier-restrictions-and-guidelines/), [package FAQ](https://help.uber.com/en-AU/riders/article/package-delivery-faq?nodeId=17a6d437-bceb-4d74-8d54-fe4f10f89d81)). A framed canvas is definitionally fragile: **unusable**.
- **Uber Direct (merchant white-label API):** car deliveries up to **2m³ (~175×130×90cm)** and 20kg/item, value cap **$500 unless a written agreement** exists ([order size guidelines](https://help.uber.com/en/merchants-and-restaurants/article/order-size-guidelines-uber-direct?nodeId=d5702d8b-665a-4c3c-8c25-04f157c312e3), [Direct API](https://developer.uber.com/docs/deliveries/overview)). A 60×90 canvas *fits physically* and the API is excellent — but drivers are untrained, may cancel bulky jobs, and no fragile/damage cover is offered. Verdict: **not viable for artwork**; keep on the radar only if Uber ever writes a bespoke agreement.

### 1.3 DoorDash Drive AU — wrong pipe ❌

White-label Dasher dispatch, **base $8.50 ex GST first km + $1/km to 5km**, servicing towns >30,000 population ([OrderUp/DoorDash Drive flow](https://support.orderup.com.au/en/articles/9698965-doordash-drive-merchant-customer-flow), [Drive On-Demand AU](https://merchants.doordash.com/en-au/products/drive-on-demand)). Built for food/grocery; AU size limits unpublished; sedan/bike fleet; no artwork handling or cover. Cheap and API-rich, but wrong network.

### 1.4 Sherpa — the one credible on-demand API partner ⚠️

- **Status:** operating and consolidated — **Drive Yello merged into Sherpa on 26 March 2025** (Yello platform closed 31 July 2025), creating "Australia's largest independent same-day delivery network" ([merger announcement](https://blog.sherpa.net.au/drive-yello-merges-with-sherpa)). 140k+ monthly deliveries, 98% on-time claimed ([Sherpa for Business](https://www.sherpa.net.au/for-business)).
- **SEQ coverage:** Brisbane and **Gold Coast as major metros; Sunshine Coast and Toowoomba served with a 15% non-major-metro loading** ([surcharges](https://help.auuser.sherpadelivery.com/hc/en-us/articles/4409751988621-Are-there-any-delivery-surcharges-or-additional-costs), [for-business coverage list](https://www.sherpa.net.au/for-business)) — the only gig network that maps onto AccessArt's whole triangle.
- **Vehicles/items:** bike, car, van tiers; car jobs include 2 items, van 4, extra-item fees beyond that; weight surcharges over 40kg. Packaging guidelines require senders to wrap fragile items properly ("fragile" labels alone are insufficient) and refuse only *extremely* fragile items like tiered cakes — **a properly wrapped framed canvas is not prohibited**, unlike Zoom2u.
- **Pricing:** dynamic per-quote; base rates unpublished (7am–9pm, 7 days). No verifiable Brisbane figure for a canvas-sized car job — expect same-day car jobs in the **$25–$60** band based on its market position between GoPeople's $8.95 small-parcel product and Zoom2u's $60+ Brisbane quotes, but **treat this as unverified until quoted**.
- **Insurance:** not published for the AU consumer service; "high-value goods" handling and code-verified POD are advertised, but assume **no art-specific damage cover** without a written business agreement.
- **API:** yes — business API link-up and e-commerce plugins ([for-business](https://www.sherpa.net.au/for-business), [Shopify app](https://apps.shopify.com/sherpa-delivery)).

### 1.5 GoPeople — cheap, API-ready, but art is excluded from cover ⚠️

- **Products:** GoSAMEDAY store-to-door **from $8.95 ex GST** (Sydney flat rates $9.95 <10km / $13.95 10–20km as the published benchmark), GoNOW on-demand, and **GoSHIFT hourly driver hire (min 3 hours, choice of vehicle incl. van)** ([GoSAMEDAY](https://www.gopeople.com.au/gosameday/), [prices](https://www.gopeople.com.au/courier-prices/), [GoSHIFT how-to](https://www.gopeople.com.au/knowledgebase/how-to-goshift-create-a-shift/)). Brisbane covered ([Brisbane page](https://www.gopeople.com.au/courier-companies-brisbane/)).
- **The disqualifier for per-parcel use:** deliveries include $250 FreightSafe warranty cover, but the FreightSafe terms **exclude "works of art"** and glass products from cover entirely ([FreightSafe terms](https://www.gopeople.com.au/freightsafe-terms/), [warranty program](https://www.gopeople.com.au/freightsafe-about/)). GoVIP's warranty is likewise capped at $100 ([GoNOW/GoVIP sender terms](https://www.gopeople.com.au/terms-for-senders-govip/)). So art rides at owner's risk.
- **Where it still fits:** **GoSHIFT hourly van hire is the interesting product** — hire a vetted driver+van for a 3–4 hour batched swap run, with AccessArt (not the parcel warranty) carrying the goods risk under its own policy. Hourly rates vary by vehicle and aren't published; get a quote.
- **API:** yes — documented member API (key on request) plus AfterShip and Shopify integrations ([docs.gopeople.com.au](http://docs.gopeople.com.au/), [AfterShip carrier page](https://www.aftership.com/carriers/gopeople/api)).

### 1.6 Drive Yello — defunct as a separate option ❌

Merged into Sherpa March 2025; platform shut 31 July 2025 ([Sherpa announcement](https://blog.sherpa.net.au/drive-yello-merges-with-sherpa)). Any older research citing Yello should redirect to Sherpa.

### 1.7 "Man with a van" owner-driver marketplaces ✅

- **Little Green Truck** — national franchise of owner-drivers ("delivery truck hire with a friendly driver **from $30**", pay on completion, no deposit), with Brisbane-area operators including Brisbane South, Fortitude Valley and Coomera ([littlegreentruck.com.au](https://littlegreentruck.com.au/), [Brisbane South](https://littlegreentruck.com.au/in-your-area/queensland/brisbane-south/)). Franchisees are ABN owner-operators — exactly the profile of a future AccessArt agent. Insurance per-franchisee; confirm goods-in-transit and art handling when engaging (unverified).
- **The Handy Truck** — same model, "man and a ute" in Brisbane City ([handytruck.com.au](https://www.handytruck.com.au/areas/qld/brisbane-city/services/man-and-a-ute)). Quote-based.

Prices are historic; useful as clearing-price evidence for ad-hoc art moves (~$100–$300), not as a bookable rate card.
- **Kerb** (Brisbane-founded parking/storage space marketplace, still operating — [kerb.works Brisbane](https://kerb.works/australia/brisbane/spaces)) is **not a delivery option**; at most it's overflow storage near venues. Noted and parked.

## 2. Two-person / white-glove home delivery networks

These are **contract 3PLs, not bookable per-job services** — they take third-party work only as retained retail accounts with committed volume.

- **ANC** ([ancdelivers.com.au](https://ancdelivers.com.au/)) — last-mile partner for IKEA, Bunnings, JB Hi-Fi, Temple & Webster, Williams Sonoma and Harvey Norman; 1,000+ delivery professionals, two-person teams, in-home placement and assembly ([furniture page](https://ancdelivers.com.au/sending/furniture-and-homewares)). No public rate card; the retail proxy is IKEA's customer-facing pricing delivered by partners like ANC: **$49–$89 to-the-door for large orders, "In The Home" two-person service from $39 plus** ([IKEA AU delivery](https://www.ikea.com/au/en/customer-service/services/delivery/)). Read that as **~$50–$130 per contracted drop at retail scale** — achievable for AccessArt only with hundreds of drops/week.
- **Winning Services** ([winningservices.com.au/delivery](https://www.winningservices.com.au/delivery)) — Winning Group's logistics arm (Appliances Online etc.), offering delivery + installation and 3PL to external partners across AU/NZ. Quote/contract only.
- **HDS** ([hdsau.com](https://www.hdsau.com/)) — 3PL with 10 DCs and 650 vehicles, home delivery incl. temperature-controlled. Quote/contract only; appliance/FMCG-centric.

**Verdict:** irrelevant at Stage 1–2 volumes; a Stage 3+ conversation if AccessArt ever needs guaranteed two-person in-home hanging service at hundreds of moves per week. None are fine-art handlers.

## 4. The "delivery agent" as a platform role — legal and structural reality

### 4.1 Vetting norms to copy

- **Airtasker:** platform-verified ID; optional **ACIC national police check** badge (Sterling RISQ, clean-record, point-in-time); licence/qualification badges; public ratings and completion history; on-platform escrowed payment as the enforcement lever ([badges](https://www.airtasker.com/blog/airtasker-badges/)).
- **Uber AU:** mandatory **Nationally Coordinated Criminal History Check** via National Crime Check (Uber-funded); full AU licence held 12+ months (P2 accepted for car *deliveries* in QLD); driver must be listed on the vehicle's insurance; vehicle age/ANCAP standards ([Uber requirements](https://www.uber.com/au/en/drive/requirements/), [background check](https://www.uber.com/au/en/drive/requirements/background-check/), [NCC](https://www.nationalcrimecheck.com.au/uber)).
- **AccessArt agent minimum (mirroring the above):** ABN + GST status; full driver's licence; vehicle rego + comprehensive insurance **with business-use endorsement**; national police check (platform-funded, refreshed ~24-monthly); evidence of public liability ($10m norm) and goods-in-transit cover; a 30-minute art-handling induction (blanket wrap, vertical carry, no flat-stacking, glass-tape); star ratings + damage-strike system.

### 4.2 Australian law, precisely, as at 30 August 2026 — ⚠️ get professional advice before Stage 3

- **The regime:** the *Fair Work Legislation Amendment (Closing Loopholes No. 2) Act 2024* created (from 26 Aug 2024) a category of **"employee-like workers"** — independent contractors performing digital-platform work who exhibit at least two of: low bargaining power, pay at/below comparable employees, low authority over how work is performed ([Fair Work Ombudsman](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/find-help-for/independent-contractors/regulated-workers/employee-like-workers)). The FWC can now set binding **minimum standards orders (MSOs)** for them, and workers gained **unfair-deactivation** protection; the **Digital Labour Platform Deactivation Code** has required fair process before deactivating an employee-like worker since **26 February 2025** ([Ministers' media release](https://ministers.dewr.gov.au/watt/better-workplace-protections-rideshare-food-delivery-and-truck-drivers)).
- **The first MSO just landed:** the Expert Panel made the **Interim On-Demand Delivery Employee-like Worker MSO on 11 August 2026, operative 17 August 2026** — minimum hourly rates of **$31.30** (bicycle/e-bike/scooter/foot), **$31.50** (motorbike) and **$32.00** (car or van up to 1-tonne capacity), an earnings-floor top-up over periods up to 21 days, a gig-worker information statement, record-keeping, insurance and expense terms, consultation and dispute-resolution rights ([FWC](https://www.fwc.gov.au/about-us/news-and-media/news/minimum-standards-order-and-decision-made), [FWO summary](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/about-us/workplace-laws/fair-work-commission-orders/minimum-standards-order-on-demand-delivery-workers), [HCA Mag rates](https://www.hcamag.com/au/specialisation/benefits/safety-net-delivery-riders-to-get-minimum-hourly-rates-starting-next-week/585777)). Rates rise $0.50 from 1 January 2027.
- **Does it catch AccessArt?** The interim order covers on-demand delivery of **food, drinks, alcohol and groceries** and excludes vehicles over 1-tonne capacity — artwork delivery is *outside* its scope today. **But** the TWU's parallel applications (MS2024/1–3) seeking MSOs for **"last-mile" delivery work and road transport contractors** are before the Commission and the FWC has flagged it will revisit the interim order when they progress ([FWC major case page](https://fwc.gov.au/hearings-decisions/major-cases/minimum-standards-orders-ms20241-ms20242-ms20243)). A last-mile MSO would very plausibly cover an AccessArt job feed.
- **The dividing line that matters:** if AccessArt **merely lets ops or users book third-party services** (Airtasker task, GoPeople booking, panel courier), the *other* platform or the courier carries the platform obligations — AccessArt is a customer. The moment AccessArt **operates its own app-based job feed, sets pay, and pays independent contractors for delivery work**, it is arguably a "digital labour platform operator": deactivation-code process obligations, exposure to unfair-deactivation claims (workers regularly engaged 6+ months), future MSO coverage, plus ordinary contractor law (sham-contracting provisions, unfair contract terms for contractors, state workers'-comp edge cases and CTP/business-use insurance questions). **None of this is fatal — Uber and Airtasker operate inside it — but it is a compliance step-change that must be priced and lawyered before Stage 3.** This section is research, not legal advice.

### 4.3 Insurance structure

- **Agent-held (Stage 1–2):** an SEQ owner-driver's typical package — comprehensive motor + $10m public liability — runs **~$1,200–$1,800/yr**, rising to **~$1,500–$2,200/yr with $10k/load goods-in-transit** ([National Cover courier insurance guide](https://nationalcover.com.au/courier-insurance-australia/)). Caveat: standard GIT wordings frequently exclude or restrict fine art/glass — agents' certificates must be read, not just collected.
- **Platform blanket (Stage 2–3):** brokered fine-art transit/exhibition cover in Australia prices at roughly **1.5–2.5% of insured value per event** ([NAVA transit & exhibition insurance factsheet](https://visualarts.net.au/media/uploads/files/Transit_and_Exhibition_Insurance_Factsheet.pdf)), while annual scheduled collection policies for well-managed collections run nearer **0.1–0.3% of value p.a.** (broker guidance collated at [Insurance Business AU](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/au/guides/art-insurance-517617.aspx)); prior AccessArt research's 0.3–1.5% ad-valorem estimate sits between these and remains the planning band. For a $600 average work, per-move ad-valorem cover is $2–$15 — affordable *if* bought as an annual platform policy over declared movements rather than per-event. Public liability: venues will expect $10–$20m certificates from anyone carrying art through their premises.

## 5. Batched "trail run" economics — the swap-day van route

**The precedent is already on the road:** TED/IAS runs fixed-schedule art shuttles precisely because consolidation is "a cost-effective option to move single works" ([TED](https://www.tedfineart.com.au/art-transport/)), and Artwork Transport runs **daily Brisbane metro routes** ([artworktransport.com.au](https://artworktransport.com.au/)). The same route-density logic powers meal-prep and commercial laundry runs: fixed weekly circuit, many drops, one vehicle.

**Cost model** (anchors: Airtasker two-person removalist rate $90–$120/hr ([furniture removal guide](https://www.airtasker.com/removals/furniture-removal/price/furniture-removalist-cost/)); the new FWC $32/hr car/van wage floor as the statutory signal for solo gig driving; GoPeople GoSHIFT minimum 3-hour van hire). Assume a solo agent + van, all-in charge-out of **$55–$75/hr** (wage floor + vehicle running + margin), on a 4-hour Saturday circuit of 6–8 venues:

| Run cost (4h) | 6 artworks | 10 artworks | 16 artworks | 24 artworks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $220 (@$55/hr) | $37/work | $22/work | $14/work | $9/work |
| $300 (@$75/hr) | $50/work | $30/work | $19/work | $13/work |

Sixteen artworks is one full venue-swap circuit (8 venues × 2 movements each); at that density the per-move cost is **$14–$19 versus ~$100+ per-job gig pricing** — an ~85% saving, and the only structure under which a $15–$30/week rental margin survives paid delivery. Below ~6 moves/run, per-job Airtasker is cheaper; that is the crossover volume at which a scheduled run should start. Caveat: the $55–$75/hr charge-out is a modelled figure, not a quoted rate — validate with a GoSHIFT quote and a Little Green Truck operator before committing.

## 6. Staged operating recommendation

1. **Stage 1 (launch, <6 moves/week): ops-booked per-job.** Default remains venue-node self-service (doc 06); when a paid move is needed, ops posts to **Airtasker** (budget $60–$110) or books a **Little Green Truck / Handy Truck** owner-driver (~$50–$100), with **Burl By Design ($80 min) or Professional Picture Hanging** for works >$1,000. Artwork travels wrapped to AccessArt's packing standard; risk sits on AccessArt's own goods policy, not the carrier's.
2. **Stage 1 software:** even now, record every movement: `custody_event` (from_party → to_party, timestamp, booking ref, agent name/platform), **condition photos at check-out and check-in** (front, back, corners), wrapped-state photo, and POD photo + recipient name. This is the damage-dispute spine and it must predate any agent network.
3. **Stage 2 (6–30 moves/week): preferred-agent panel.** Sign 2–4 named agents (an Artwork Transport metro slot, Burl By Design, one or two vetted owner-drivers) on agreed per-move and per-run rates; collect and diarise **insurance certificates (comprehensive + $10m PL + GIT, art exclusions checked)**, police checks and ABNs. Start the **weekly swap-day trail run** the week volume clears ~6 moves; target $15–$25/artwork.
4. **Stage 2 software adds:** agent registry (vetting docs + expiry alerts), run manifests (ordered stop list, per-stop pick/drop), per-artwork scan/photo at each hand-off, damage check-in/out flags that block re-listing until resolved, and declared-value capture feeding the insurance schedule.
5. **Insurance before scale:** buy the **annual platform transit policy** (0.3–1.5% ad-valorem planning band; NAVA benchmarks 1.5–2.5% per event for one-offs) once monthly declared movement value makes it cheaper than per-event cover; require venues-facing agents to show $10m PL regardless.
6. **Stage 3 (30+ moves/week, only if panel capacity or cost breaks): in-app `delivery_agent` role** — a job feed of runs and per-job moves, agent onboarding flow (ABN, licence, police check, insurance upload, art-handling induction), acceptance, live status, in-app POD.
7. **The legal threshold Stage 3 crosses:** operating a job feed that pays independent contractors makes AccessArt a **digital labour platform operator** under the Closing Loopholes regime — Deactivation Code process duties, unfair-deactivation exposure, and probable coverage by a future last-mile MSO (TWU applications pending; food-delivery MSO already sets $32/hr for car/van work). **Obtain employment-law advice and cost the $32/hr-equivalent floor into run pricing before building this.**
8. **Stage 3 software adds:** agent payment ledger and RCTI invoicing, deactivation workflow with human review and notice (Code-compliant), earnings-period tracking against any MSO floor, incident/damage claim module tied to the insurance policy, and agent ratings visible to ops (not renters).
9. **Never build on:** Uber Connect/Courier (fragile prohibited), GoPeople per-parcel for art (cover excluded), DoorDash Drive (wrong network), Drive Yello (defunct). Keep Sherpa warm as the API-based overflow option once a written business agreement addresses cover.
10. **Review trigger:** re-check the FWC last-mile MSO applications (MS2024/1–3) each quarter — if a last-mile order lands, Stage 2 panel contracts may also need review depending on how "digital labour platform" boundaries are drawn.
